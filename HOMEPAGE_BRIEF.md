# JR Dog Training — Homepage Brief

Status: first working brief based on the live Mad Paws listing, checked 6 October 2026.

## Core position

**Practical dog training for calmer everyday life.**

JR Dog Training helps owners in Morisset and surrounding areas understand the routines, reactions, and mixed messages that may be keeping everyday behaviour problems going. The service focuses on realistic changes that owners can apply at home and on walks.

The site should be especially clear that James is a self-taught trainer with ten years of practical experience, not a certified dog behaviourist or veterinary behaviourist. This honesty is part of the trust proposition, not small print.

## Primary audience

Local owners who are struggling with a dog at home or on walks, particularly with:

- Leash pulling
- Excessive barking
- Overexcitement
- Household boundaries and routines
- Mild anxiety
- Lower-level reactive behaviour

Dogs of any breed, age, or size may be considered. Dogs presenting severe behaviour or safety risks may need specialist support.

## Homepage narrative

### 1. Recognition

Heading: **It is rarely just one command.**

Copy direction:

When a dog keeps pulling, barking, becoming overexcited, or reacting on walks, the answer may involve more than teaching another cue. Look at the full picture: the dog's history, home environment, daily routines, and the way people and dog respond to each other.

Purpose: make owners feel understood without diagnosing their dog or promising a quick fix.

### 2. What James can help with

Use six concise problem cards:

- Pulling on the lead
- Excessive barking
- Overexcitement and difficulty settling
- Household boundaries and routines
- Mild anxiety
- Lower-level reactive behaviour

Boundary note:

> Not sure whether your dog's needs are within scope? Contact James through Mad Paws before booking. He will be upfront if a certified or veterinary behaviour specialist is the safer next step.

### 3. How it works

1. **Tell James what is happening** — Share your dog's history, living situation, the behaviour you are seeing, and what you would like to change through Mad Paws.
2. **Meet at your home** — James observes the dog in its everyday environment and looks for routines or interactions that may be contributing.
3. **Put practical changes into action** — Use the one-hour session to begin realistic steps that the household can apply consistently.

### 4. Service card

Heading: **One-hour training visit at your home**

- For dogs of all breeds and sizes
- **$112 for a one-hour house visit**
- Add **$15** when travel time from Bonnells Bay exceeds 30 minutes
- House visits only; no sessions at the trainer's home
- Current availability and booking handled through Mad Paws

Button: **View availability on Mad Paws**

### 5. About James

Draft:

> I have spent the past ten years training my own dogs and helping friends and local owners with theirs. I share my life with two German Shepherd–Koolie crosses, Merlin and Nimue, whom local dog owners may have seen out and about with me.
>
> My experience includes working with dogs presenting serious behavioural and safety challenges, as well as helping owners who feel stuck with everyday problems at home or on walks. My approach is practical: understand what is keeping the problem going, then make changes that can work in real life.
>
> I am a self-taught trainer, not a certified dog behaviourist or veterinary behaviourist. I am transparent about the limits of my experience and will recommend specialist support where a dog's needs or safety risks fall outside my scope.

The third paragraph should be visibly presented, not hidden in fine print.

### 6. Early-client proof

Do not display an empty review score or invent testimonials. Until client feedback is available, use a small “Building JR Dog Training locally” section that explains the personalised, early-stage service.

After the first engagements, replace or supplement it with specific, permission-backed feedback covering:

- The problem before training
- What James helped the owner understand or change
- A concrete improvement the owner noticed
- First name/dog name/photo only to the extent authorised
